For nearly three decades, digital marketing has had a single playbook: write content, get links, and rank on page one of Google so users click your website link.

But a massive shift is happening. Your customers are changing how they search. 

Instead of typing *"best dentist in flagstaff"* into Google and clicking five different links, they are opening ChatGPT, Claude, Perplexity, or talking to Siri and asking: *"I need a dentist in Flagstaff who is friendly, accepts Cigna, and has great weekend hours. Who do you recommend?"*

The AI doesn't give them a list of ten blue links. It synthesizes a conversational answer, names two dentists, cites its reasons, and **cites its sources.**

To stay visible, you need a strategy for this new era. That is where **AEO** and **GEO** come in.

---

### Understanding the Acronyms

- **AEO (Answer Engine Optimization):** Optimizing your business content to be retrieved by direct voice and text answer assistants (Siri, Alexa, Google Assistant).
- **GEO (Generative Engine Optimization):** Structuring your business information so large language models (LLMs) like ChatGPT, Gemini, and Claude select your business as an authoritative cited reference in their conversational recommendations.

---

### How LLMs Pick Which Businesses to Recommend

AI models do not just guess. They crawl the web, search engine database records, public reviews, and high-trust citation listings to determine which businesses are credible. 

To make sure your business is cited inside AI search outputs, you need a three-pronged approach:

1. **Topical Specificity over Generic Filler:** AI search engines reward depth and unique answers. If your plumbing website just says *"we do plumbing services,"* you'll be ignored. If you have detailed pages answering niche questions like *"how to prevent frozen water pipes in Sedona winter climates,"* the AI will cite your expert page.
2. **Review Sentiment & Detail:** LLMs parse public reviews to evaluate your reputation. They don't just look at a 4.8-star average; they read customer details. High-frequency keywords in your reviews (e.g., *"honest pricing,"* *"professional service"*) give the AI the confidence to call you *"the most reliable electrician in Prescott."*
3. **Structured Data Passports:** Injected JSON Schema gives AI systems direct, machine-readable facts about your business, prices, and service areas.
