For a small business in Northern or Western Arizona—whether you’re running a plumbing business in Prescott, managing cabin rentals in Flagstaff, or operating jeep tours in Sedona—traditional national SEO advice is a waste of your money. 

You aren't trying to rank globally. You need to dominate your immediate backyard. When someone in Mohave Valley searches for *"electrician near me"*, they are ready to buy. If your competitor shows up in the Google Maps 3-Pack and you don’t, they get the phone call.

Here are three non-negotiable local SEO pillars every Arizona business must implement immediately:

---

### 1. Consistent NAP (Name, Address, Phone Number)
Search engines scan the web for matching signals. If your Yelp profile lists your business as *"Sedona Auto Repair"*, your Facebook page says *"Sedona Automotive Repair LLC"*, and your website has a third variation, search engines get confused and lower your trust.

Make sure your **Name, Address, and Phone Number (928-xxx-xxxx)** are character-for-character identical across:
- Your core website footer.
- Your Google Business Profile.
- Yelp, Bing Places, and Apple Maps.
- Local Arizona business directories.

---

### 2. Maximize Your Google Business Profile (GBP)
Your GBP is the single most valuable free asset for local ranking. 
- **Reviews with Location Keywords:** Don't just ask clients for *"a review."* Prompt them to say what service they got and where: *"928Tech built a fantastic website for my cabinet shop in Prescott!"*
- **Post Weekly Updates:** Google treats GBP like a social platform. Upload photos of recent jobs, list new services, and post short updates.
- **Complete the Q&A Section:** You can post your own questions and answers! List the top 5 questions clients call and ask, and provide professional answers.

---

### 3. Embed LocalBusiness Schema Markup
Search engine bots are smart, but they love structured data. **LocalBusiness Schema** is a block of JSON code injected behind your website that explicitly tells search engines:
- *"This is our local telephone number."*
- *"This is our precise geo-latitude and longitude."*
- *"These are our opening hours."*
