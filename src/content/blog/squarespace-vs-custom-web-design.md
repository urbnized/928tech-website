It’s an attractive pitch: *"Build your business website in an afternoon for $25 a month using our simple template builder!"* 

Every year, thousands of Arizona business owners sign up for Squarespace, Wix, or Shopify. But while these proprietary drag-and-drop platforms are easy to start with, they come with a high hidden cost that actively holds your local business back.

Here is the truth about what happens when you "rent" a generic builder template, and why investing in clean custom code is the fairest path for long-term growth:

---

### 1. Bloated Code = Painfully Slow Speeds
Generic page builders have to support every feature under the sun. To make drag-and-drop work, they load massive libraries of unseen code, javascript plugins, and styling rules.

When a customer in Prescott or Sedona searches for your service on a mobile phone with a weak 4G connection, a template site can take **4 to 8 seconds** to load.
- According to Google, **53% of mobile visits are abandoned** if a page takes longer than 3 seconds to load.
- Uptime speeds directly impact SEO. Google penalizes slow websites under their "Core Web Vitals" standard.

At **928Tech**, we build using **Astro**, a modern custom coding framework that compiles your website into 100% clean, static HTML. There is zero bloated javascript sent to the browser. Our sites load in **under 0.5 seconds**, achieving perfect 100/100 performance scores.

---

### 2. Lack of True SEO & Schema Customization
Drag-and-drop platforms have extremely rigid SEO capabilities. They generate generic markup, make it difficult to customize advanced JSON-LD structured data (critical for modern AEO/GEO AI search), and often produce messy heading hierarchies (`<h1>` tags) that confuse Google's indexing bots.

To rank in competitive local keywords, you need surgical precision in your codebase. A custom-coded Astro website lets us tailor every tag, meta tag, and Schema indicator to your exact service categories.

---

### 3. Proprietary Platform Lock-In (Hostage Domains)
What happens if you want to move your Squarespace site to a different hosting provider because their monthly rates suddenly doubled? 

**You can't.** 

Your website files are proprietary code locked inside their platform. If you cancel your subscription, your website is deleted, and you have to rebuild from scratch. Often, they make transferring your domain name a nightmare.
